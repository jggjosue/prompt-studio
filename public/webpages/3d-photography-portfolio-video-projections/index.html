3D Photography Portfolio with Video Projections on Surfaces

Build an elegant, silent 3D photography portfolio with video projections on fabric panels and walls.

**Deliverables:** index.html, styles.css, photo3d.js, assets/ folder.

**Stack:** Three.js, video textures, CSS grid for UI.

**Description:** 3D surfaces (fabric panels, walls) that project photographic videos; hovering expands the projection and the camera performs a smooth pan. Elegant and silent aesthetic.

**Key interactions:** Hover to expand, click for fullscreen, clip scrubbing.

**Accessibility & fallback:** 2D gallery with captions and subtitles.

**Recommended assets:** 4–12s clips, fabric textures, panel models.
