3D Art Museum with Guided Curation and Video Projections

Build a 3D curated art museum where each artwork has a curatorial video explaining context, technique, and anecdotes. The visitor can activate a guided curation that plays a video sequence and moves the camera with elegant transitions.

**Deliverables:** index.html, styles.css, museum.js, assets/ folder with curatorial videos, artwork models, and metadata.

**Stack:** Three.js, HTML5 video, GSAP, search index.

**Description:** A 3D curated museum tour where each artwork features a curatorial video. The visitor can activate a guided curation that plays a sequenced video tour with camera transitions. Free explore mode also available.

**Video & metadata presentation:** Side panel with transcript and technical sheet; video in 3D panel or overlay. Language options and subtitles; downloadable catalog PDF.

**Curatorial interactions:** "Curator" mode with voiceover and chapter markers; "Explore" free mode. Favorites and personal notes saved locally.

**Accessibility:** Subtitles, artwork descriptions for visually impaired, ARIA on controls.
