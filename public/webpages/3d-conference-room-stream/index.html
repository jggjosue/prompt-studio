3D Light Mode Conference Room with Streaming and Highlight Clips

A light mode conference room with cream seats, a large central screen, and side panels for highlight clips. The main stream plays on the central screen via HLS; highlight clips play on side panels as 3D textures or HTML overlays. The camera offers predefined angles: stage, audience, and side view; the user can switch with smooth transitions.

During the event, moderators can mark key moments that generate highlight clips; these appear in a side list and can be replayed with one click. Live captions when supported. A 'replay' function plays the selected segment with free camera and markers. Social reactions subtly affect lighting and particles.

Performance: adaptive buffering, low resolution on mobile, pauses 3D render when tab is hidden. 2D-only stream option for slow connections.

**Deliverables:** index.html, styles.css, conference3d.js, assets/ (HLS streams, highlight clips, room models, SRT, transcripts).

**Stack:** Three.js, HLS.js, video textures, postprocessing.

**Interactions:** Camera angle switching, clip replay, real-time reactions, 2D stream fallback.
