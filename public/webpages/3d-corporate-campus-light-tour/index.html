3D Light Mode Corporate Campus Tour with Explanatory Videos

Build a light mode 3D corporate campus tour with a luminous minimal palette, pale gold and sky blue accents, and soft global HDRI daylight illumination. The GLB campus model is segmented by semantic zones: lobby, auditorium, lab, terrace, cafeteria. Each zone has a translucent spherical 3D hotspot with subtle border and projected shadow. Camera starts in a wide aerial view with a floating "Start tour" button.

**Deliverables:** index.html, styles.css, app.js, assets/ folder with GLB models, HDR textures, MP4 videos, SRT subtitles.

**Stack:** Three.js (CDN), GSAP for transitions, HTML5 video as textures, Draco for GLB compression, IntersectionObserver for lazy-load.

**Description:** A bright, minimal 3D tour of a corporate headquarters with 5 semantic zones. Clicking a hotspot triggers a cinematic fly-to (GSAP, 800-1200ms) and loads an explanatory video in a semi-transparent white overlay panel with rounded corners. Video includes synchronized SRT subtitles and a toggle between 3D texture projection and HTML overlay. Animated metrics appear on the hotspot during playback.

**Interactions:** Drag to rotate, scroll/pinch to zoom, number keys 1-5 for predefined views. Auto-tour mode with voiceover and chapter markers. Progressive video loading (WebP thumbnails, 720p H.264, 480p fallback). LOD for buildings, culling for vegetation.

**Accessibility & fallback:** SRT subtitles, downloadable transcript, role="dialog" on video panel, prefers-reduced-motion reduces fly-to. 2D fallback with hero video and chapters.
