3D Corporate Library — Training and onboarding pro

A training and onboarding library with structured playlists, clips, and internal search. A training-focused corporate library with playlists, onboarding clips, and search. Enhanced corporate library in light mode with shelves organized by department (IT, RH, Finances, Marketing). Each training clip has metadata and a panel with video, subtitles, timestamped notes, and completion tracking. Algolia-like full-text search with relevance ranking. Filters by duration, department, and level. Fly-to camera on clip selection.

Timestamped notes saved in IndexedDB, exportable to TXT. Playlist system for curating training sequences. Scrubbing with proxy thumbnail previews. Adaptive streaming. Accessibility: subtitles, transcripts, prefers-reduced-motion support, 2D fallback view. Metrics: completion rate, average time per clip, most frequent notes. LOD and lazy-load per shelf.

**Deliverables:** index.html, styles.css, corpLibraryPro.js, assets/ (metadata JSON, WebP thumbnails).

**Stack:** Three.js, video HTML5, Algolia-like local search engine, GSAP, IndexedDB.

**Interactions:** Semantic search and fly-to, timestamped notes, training playlists, scrubbing with proxy previews, completion tracking.
