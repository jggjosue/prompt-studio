3D Light Mode Data Visualizer with Video Insights and Comparator

A 3D dashboard in light mode with floating widgets on a light plane. Each widget is a 3D object showing a visualization; selecting it plays a video insight that explains the trend. Two periods can be compared by activating comparator mode: the camera visually splits and plays two synced videos side by side while visualizations animate differences with particles and interpolated lines.

Insight videos are 60-180s narrations with subtitles and embedded graphics; playing them expands the widget and shows 3D details. Synchronized scrubbing between video and data visualization; scrubbing interpolates data and updates 3D animations in real time. Live data via WebSocket with smoothing to prevent jumps.

Controls for exporting snapshots and generating short insight clips with timestamps. Performance: widget instancing, 512px textures, on-demand video loading.

**Deliverables:** index.html, styles.css, data3d.js, assets/ (insight videos, JSON datasets, widget models, SRT, transcripts).

**Stack:** Three.js, D3.js, video HTML5, WebSocket.

**Interactions:** Widget selection plays insight, synchronized comparator, scrubbing updates visualization.
