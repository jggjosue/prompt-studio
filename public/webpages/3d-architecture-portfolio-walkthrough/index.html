3D Architecture Portfolio — Client review route

A client-review portfolio structured as a presentation route for plans, scope, and before/after decisions. A concept-led architecture route that emphasizes narrative, spatial rhythm, and design exploration. A client-facing walkthrough portfolio built to present spaces, plans, and before/after stories. Build a 3D architectural portfolio with guided walkthroughs, project videos combining real footage with renders, and downloadable plans.

**Deliverables:** index.html, styles.css, arch3d.js, assets/ folder with GLB building models, walkthrough videos, and PDF plans.

**Stack:** Three.js, HTML5 video, postprocessing, GSAP.

**Description:** Present architectural projects through 3D walkthroughs and project videos combining real shots with renders. The user can explore the building in 3D and, at key points, play videos showing construction, interviews, and technical details.

**Video & plans integration:** Videos play in 3D panels or HTML overlays; plans and sections displayed as downloadable PDFs. Each point of interest has an asset package: video, photos, plan, and spec sheet.

**Navigation controls:** Guided walkthrough with camera following a route; free mode with WASD/drag. Hotspots with microvideos and notes; "before/after" slider option.

**Visual fidelity & performance:** LOD-optimized models; HDRI for realistic lighting; subtle postprocessing. 1080p videos for desktop, 720p for mobile; adaptive streaming.

**Accessibility & documentation:** Subtitles, transcripts, and technical descriptions. ARIA on hotspots and role="dialog" for video modals.

**Metrics:** Time in walkthrough, most-visited hotspots, plan downloads, video plays.
