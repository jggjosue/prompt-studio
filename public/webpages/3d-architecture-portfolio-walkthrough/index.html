Walkthrough Process

A clear production flow from references to a polished interactive presentation.

  1. 01

    Concept & References

    Define atmosphere, project goals, architectural cues, and visual direction.

  2. 02

    3D Modeling

    Build clean architecture, context, interior volumes, and presentation geometry.

  3. 03

    Materials & Lighting

  4. 04

    Interactive Walkthrough

    Connect camera paths, hotspots, project details, and guided navigation.

  5. 05

    Final Presentation

    Deliver optimized web, render, and pitch-ready assets for client review.
