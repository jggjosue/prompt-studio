3D Corporate Timeline with Animated Dioramas and Video Narration

Build a 3D corporate timeline with elegant animated dioramas and narrated video at each milestone.

**Deliverables:** index.html, styles.css, timeline3d.js, assets/ folder.

**Stack:** Three.js, HTML5 video, ScrollTrigger.

**Description:** 3D timeline with elegant dioramas; upon reaching each milestone a narrated video plays and the camera pans with cinematic easing. Serif typography for titles.

**Key interactions:** Keyboard navigation between milestones, timeline scrubbing, subtitles.

**Accessibility & fallback:** Transcripts and linear version with videos.

**Recommended assets:** 60–120s narrated clips, diorama models.
